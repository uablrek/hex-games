^arrows - scroll map (or drag it)

|Order of Play|

The side with the highest combined BRP goes first. Germany and Italy
is always combined, even when Italy i neutral. US is included spring
1942, and USSR when war is declared.
^
^You must select the first player manually. In PvP games, the
Axis player makes the choice

|Declaration of War (DoW)|

Click on a land to declare war. The other player will deploy minor
country forces.
^
^The game doesn't know about DoW, so this is only about
deployment. Major countries, and their controlled minor countries,
are ignored, as well as neutral countries without forces.
^
^The Axis player always deploys the minor allies. So, to activate a
minor ally, the Axis player may "declare war" on it in this phase.

|Movement|

Drag any unit
^
^The game will not check if the move is legal (but your opponent in
PvP will)

|Unit Construction|

Drag units from the UnitBox to the map
^
^No bookkeeping is made by the game. You must keep track of
BRP yourself outside the game

|Strategic Redeployment (SR)|

Drag any units
^
^Limits:
^Germany - 9
^Italy - 5
^Britain - 7
^France - 5
^USSR -6
^USA - 10

|Remove units|

Click on units, and hit 'd' to remove them
^
^Unsupplied, and overstacked units are removed here (after Unit
Construction)
^
^Units owned by the non-active player may be deleted as result of
conquest of a country.
^
If France is conquered, remove half of the factors in main France
rounded up. These become Vichy forces in the next step.

|Control|

The active player may take control over countries (including colonies)
by clicking on them.

|Attrition resolution|

On all fronts where Attrition was selected
^
^The active player may remove units by selecting them and hit 'd'

|Offensive Option|

You may skip the Offensive sequence by pressing 's'
^
^Both players may delete any unit during the offensive sequence.
The active player, which is the defender during defensive missions,
may drag any friendly unit.
^
^To split air/naval units, click on the unit, and hit 'x'.
^
The Combat Result Table (CRT) will be shown during the offensive sequence

|Attacker naval+air|

The active player announces naval missions, and performs counter air
missions.

|Defender inteception+DAS|

The defender allocates Defensive Air Support to ground units.
^
^The defender may intecept any naval missions with air o fleets

|Sea Transport Missions|

Succesful sea transport missions are concluded, and fleets returns to
their base.

|Airborne drops|

Drop airborne units

|Combat|

Resolve ground attacks. Exploiting units are moved to breakthrough
hexes

|Exploitation|

Exploiting units move and attack. Unused air and airborne units may be
used, but no naval units.

|YSS|

Year Start Sequence
^
^The game does no bookkeeping, so this is informative.
^
^In PvP mode, the Axis is the active player, but YSS is performed by both 

|SW resolution|

Strategic Warfare Resolution

|BRP calculation|

^Germany - 50%
^Italy - 20%
^Britain - 40%
^France - 30%
^USSR - 30%
^US - 60%
