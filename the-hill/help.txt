|Welcome|
Welcome to "The Hill" game
^
^It is a test game used for development and test of various hex-game features.
It is made up by "sequences" and "steps".
^
^The game starts at 10am April 6 1806, and ends at 12am. Each turn is
15 minutes, which means 8 turns in total.
^
^The French player wins my occupying the 3 objectives before the game ends,
otherwise the English player wins.
^
^Enter, x - Continue to next step
^h - Toggle help-box

|solitarie|

You play a solitarie game, and control both sides. You can control the
die-rolls for test purposes.

|pvp|

This is a Player v.s. Player (PvP) game. The first to connect to the
game server becomes French player.

|ai|

You play against a computer opponent (AI).

|English Deployment|

The English player may drag units from the UnitBox to the map. English
unit may be deployed anywhere west of The River.
^
^l - Automatic deployment

|French Deployment|

The French player may drag units from the UnitBox to the map. French
unit may be deployed on clear terrain anywhere east of the dotted line.
^
^l - Automatic deployment

|Movement|

The active player may click on friendly units to mark all possible
target hexes. The click on a marked hex to go there. Moved units have
a red mark.
^
^r - Regret move
^l - Let the AI move

|Combat|

The active player may click enemy units to mark them as targets. Then
click on friendly units to participate in the attack. Watch the Combat
Resolution Table (CRT) for odds.
^
^a - Attack
^
^If the result is EX, the player with least points is eliminated, and
the other is requested to remove at least as many points.

|Wait for Combat|

Wait for the active player

|help|

Attack from river, or up-slope is halved. Defence in forrest is doubled.
